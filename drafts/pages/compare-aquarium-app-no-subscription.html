An aquarium app with no subscription and no account

Tankmate is a free aquarium app. You type in your water test readings (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, KH, pH, GH, temperature), and it tells you what each one means and what to do next. Unlimited tests, no account, works offline. I am the developer, so read this page as one side of the story, and check the sources.

What is free in Tankmate

  • One tank, unlimited water tests, no account, no ads.
  • A verdict for every reading: a colour, a plain-English meaning and an action.
  • Pro (£14 a year) adds more than one tank, all-parameter charts, export and per-tank reminders. The basics stay free.

What other apps charge or cap (checked 2026-10-24)

Prices and limits change, so every line below has the page it came from and the date it was read. Check the store page before you rely on it.

  • AquaticLog: a 1-star App Store review dated 2026-01-05 says "now it's limited to 5 measurements per month". That is one reviewer's report, not the developer's published limit. Source: App Store GB review feed, fetched 2026-10-24.
  • TankSync: a search-result summary of its listing quotes $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year with a 3-day trial. I have not re-checked this on the store page itself. Source: App Store listing, summary read 2026-10-24.
  • Tetra Digital Water Test (app name "Tetra Aquatics") is free; its advice is tied to Tetra test strips and products. Source: Practical Fishkeeping article (article undated), read 2026-10-24.
  • Aquarium Manager lists a built-in cycling coach for new tanks; it was released 2026-07-21. Source: App Store listing, read 2026-10-24.

Where Tankmate is different, and where it is not

Tankmate gives advice from fixed, written rules, and shows its reason for each. It does not have a new-tank cycling coach yet, it does not scan test strips, and it has far fewer users than the apps above. If you want a scanner or a cycling coach today, those apps do things Tankmate does not.
