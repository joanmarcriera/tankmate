Typing in readings vs scanning test strips

Some aquarium apps read a test strip or card through the phone camera. Tankmate works the other way round: you type the numbers from your liquid kit (or strips) and it tells you what to do. I am the developer of Tankmate, so this is one side, and every claim about another app below links to where I found it.

What users of two scanner apps reported

These are App Store (UK) review quotes, not my testing. They are a sample of the most recent reviews I could fetch on 2026-10-24, not a rating of the apps. Both apps may have changed since.

  • Tetra Aquatics, 1 star, 2024-04-21: "Since you updated the app it no longer allows access to the camera. The product is useless without it." Source: App Store GB review feed, fetched 2026-10-24.
  • Tetra Aquatics, 1 star, 2024-12-01: "Needs accessible to camera to perform digital tests but option not available on the app to allow camera access". Source: App Store GB review feed, fetched 2026-10-24.
  • Tetra Aquatics, 4 stars, 2023-01-30, praising the advice: "the traffic light system works well". Source: App Store GB review feed, fetched 2026-10-24.
  • The Tetra Aquatics listing showed an average of 1.69 stars from 144 ratings on 2026-10-24. Source: App Store lookup, read 2026-10-24.
  • JBL PROSCAN 2, 1 star, 2023-10-17: "Spent 2 hours trying to get this app to take a single reading". Source: App Store GB review feed, fetched 2026-10-24.
  • JBL PROSCAN 2, 2 stars, 2021-08-17: "Nitrate readings are way off every time ... Annoying there is no way to manually read the results." Source: App Store listing, review dated 2021-08-17, read 2026-10-24.

Note that the praise in the Tetra reviews is for the advice, and the complaints are about the camera. That is the gap Tankmate aims at: advice from numbers you type.

The trade-off

  • Typing is slower than pointing a camera, and you need a kit that gives you numbers.
  • A scanner can be convenient when it works; the reviews above are about when it does not.
  • Tankmate does not diagnose from photos, and it is a small app with few users.
