Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Tankmate does not collect anything. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.

Where your data lives

Every water test, note and setting you enter is stored on the device you entered it on — in your browser's local storage on the web, or inside the app's own storage on iOS. None of it is sent to a server, because Tankmate has no server to send it to.

Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, deletes your history with it. There is no backup held anywhere else, and no way for anyone (including us) to recover it.

The iOS app

The iOS app makes no network requests at all. Its entire contents are bundled inside the app, so it behaves identically with the device offline.

If you turn on the weekly water-test reminder, it is scheduled by iOS on your device. No notification is sent from a server, and no push token is registered.

The website

tankmate.joanmarcriera.es is a static site served from a server in Germany (Hetzner). The web server keeps ordinary access logs — the request, the time, and the requesting IP address — which are used only to keep the site running and secure. They are not linked to anything you enter in the app, because nothing you enter in the app is ever transmitted.

If you use the optional "Buy me a coffee" link, the checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy, who become the data controller for that payment. See their privacy policy for details. This link does not exist in the iOS app.
