Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Tankmate does not collect anything you enter. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.

Where your data lives

Every water test and note you enter is stored on the device you entered it on — in your browser's local storage on the web (under the key tankmate.tests.v1), or inside the app's own storage on iOS. On iOS the reminder day and time you choose are kept in the app's settings on the device. None of it is sent to a server: Tankmate has no backend to send it to.

On the web, the service worker also keeps a copy of the app's own files (pages, styles, script, icon) so it opens offline. That cache holds no personal data.

Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, deletes your history with it. There is no backup held anywhere else, and no way for anyone (including us) to recover it.

The iOS app

The iOS app makes no network requests at all. Its entire contents are bundled inside the app, so it behaves identically with the device offline. The "Buy me a coffee" link and the Lemon Squeezy script described below are removed from the iOS app when it is built.

If you turn on the weekly water-test reminder, it is scheduled by iOS on your device. No notification is sent from a server, and no push token is registered.

This is why the App Store privacy label for Tankmate says Data Not Collected.

The website

tankmate.joanmarcriera.es is a static site served from a server in Germany (Hetzner). The web server keeps ordinary access logs — the request, the time, and the requesting IP address — which are used only to keep the site running and secure. They are not linked to anything you enter in the app, because nothing you enter in the app is ever transmitted.

The app page loads one third-party script: Lemon Squeezy's checkout script, from app.lemonsqueezy.com, which powers the optional "Buy me a coffee" button. Because your browser fetches it on every visit to the app page, Lemon Squeezy receives the technical details any web request carries (your IP address and browser type), and their script may set its own cookies. It is never given your water tests or notes. The privacy and support pages do not load it.

If you use the "Buy me a coffee" button, the checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy, who become the data controller for that payment. See their privacy policy for details.
