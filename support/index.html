Common questions

Where is my history stored? On your device only — see the privacy page. Nothing is uploaded, so nothing can be restored if you clear your browser data or delete the app.

Does it work offline? Yes. The iOS app bundles everything it needs and makes no network requests. On the web, add Tankmate to your Home Screen and it keeps working without a signal.

The verdicts do not match my test kit's chart. Tankmate is tuned for freshwater tropical community tanks and it deliberately reacts to KH earlier than most charts do, because a thin buffer is what precedes a pH crash. If you think a rule is wrong for your setup, email me — the rules are the product, and I would rather fix them.

Multiple tanks, photos, sync? Not yet. Tankmate does one tank well on purpose. Tell me what you need and it goes on the list.
