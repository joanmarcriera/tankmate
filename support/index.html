Common questions

Where is my history stored? On your device only — see the privacy page. Nothing is uploaded, so nothing can be restored if you clear your browser data or delete the app.

Can I back up my history or move it to another device? Not yet. There is no export and no sync, so a test you log on your phone is not visible on your laptop. The history also belongs to the address you use, so the same browser on a different web address starts empty.

Can it remind me to test the water? In the iOS app, yes: tap the bell in the header to set a weekly reminder, which iOS schedules on your device. The website cannot schedule reminders on iOS, so there it is up to you.

Does it work offline? Yes. The iOS app bundles everything it needs and makes no network requests. On the web, open Tankmate once with a connection and add it to your Home Screen; after that it keeps working without a signal.

The verdicts do not match my test kit's chart. Tankmate is tuned for freshwater tropical community tanks and it deliberately reacts to KH earlier than most charts do, because a thin buffer is what precedes a pH crash. If you think a rule is wrong for your setup, email me — the rules are the product, and I would rather fix them.

Multiple tanks, photos, sync? Not yet. Tankmate does one tank well on purpose. Tell me what you need and it goes on the list.
